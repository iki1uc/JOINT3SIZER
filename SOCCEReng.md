⚽ SOCCER — Explanation (Pressure System + Spatial Logic)
Soccer is a spatial‑pressure game: two teams attempt to generate positional pressure to force a scoring event.

Core Elements:

Space (field zones, lines)

Pressure (pressing, offside, duels)

Motion (passes, runs, transitions)

Rules (17 Laws of the Game)

Time (2×45 + stoppage time)

Soccer is a dynamic pressure‑management system.

☁ WEATHER — Explanation (Energy + Pressure + Equilibrium)
Weather emerges from uneven solar heating → pressure differences → air movement.

Key Factors:

Solar input creates temperature gradients

Air pressure controls clouds & precipitation

Fronts generate weather shifts (cold front = fast, warm front = slow)

Wind equalizes pressure

Troposphere is the weather layer

Weather is a global pressure‑equilibrium system.

📈 MARKET / STOCK EXCHANGE — Explanation (Supply + Demand + Expectation)
The stock market is a price‑pressure system:

Supply & demand determine price

Companies issue shares (IPO)

Trading occurs on exchanges (NYSE, Nasdaq)

Prices react to earnings, news, expectations

The market is an expectation‑pressure system.

🏭 INDUSTRY — Explanation (Raw Material → Processing → Service → Knowledge)
Industry is a value‑transformation system with four sectors:

Primary: raw materials (agriculture, mining)

Secondary: processing/production (factories)

Tertiary: services (trade, transport)

Quaternary: knowledge/IT/R&D

Industry is a value‑transformation system.
